# Emagma, how we work

Emagma builds e-commerce and applications for a living. We have been doing it for over ten years, long enough to see what lasts and what does not.

Along the way we noticed something obvious: the tools we build and deploy have a real footprint - on the people who use them, on the teams who maintain them, and on the infrastructure that runs them. IT and AI are not neutral, and we would rather account for that than not.

So we made a few choices, not because they sound good, but because they actually produce better outcomes for the companies that hire us, and they make sense to us in our day-to-day work.

**We keep humans in the loop.** Automation is a lever, not a replacement. The people closest to the problem - merchants, developers, support teams - make better calls than any pipeline running unsupervised. When we use AI, it assists; a person decides.

**We stay in control of our tools.** AI is changing our job, and we are not pretending otherwise. We adopt it where it helps, but wherever possible we favour tools and providers that keep us - and our clients - autonomous and sovereign: over the data, over the infrastructure, and over the decision to switch away.

**We choose open source wherever it fits.** Our clients get code they can read, audit and leave with. No lock-in, no opaque licensing, no vendor that holds the keys. We aim to be replaceable, whatever the reason. When we create something reusable, we do our best to release and maintain it - this plugin is an example.

**We care about your experience with our products and services.** Your satisfaction is what we work for: we listen to your feedback and do our best to deliver a modern, respectful and polished experience.

**We work with people, not instead of them.** Our projects are staffed by a small and passionate team, and the people who scope the work are the ones who write the code. Everyone involved in a project deserves the same respect - the clients who trust us and the people who build for them. That respect starts with good communication: clear, honest and frequent, in both directions.

**We try to keep our footprint small.** Leaner infrastructure, fewer services, right-sized hosting. It costs less, it breaks less, and it wastes less. The environmental argument and the business argument happen to point in the same direction.

None of this is a manifesto. It is a set of defaults that have held up across a decade of client projects, and we are not done adjusting them. If you work with us, this is what you get.
